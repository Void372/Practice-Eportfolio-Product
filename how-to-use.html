How to use this site

A command term is the word at the start of a question that tells you what kind of answer to write.

Three steps

  1. Find your term. Pick it from the grid on the homepage.
  2. Understand it. Read what it means, what you need to do and the common mistake. Then study the example and the model answer.
  3. Practise it. Press Practise at the top of the term page, or try mixed practice.

What this site is for

This site does not teach Humanities content. It helps you show what you already know in the right way, so you do not lose marks by answering a different question from the one that was asked.

Your privacy

You do not need to log in or type your name. Your scores are not saved anywhere.
